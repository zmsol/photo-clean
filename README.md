# Photo Clean

Remove hidden tracking data from your photos before you share them.

## The problem

Every photo you take or receive can carry invisible metadata — when and where it was taken, what device captured it, and even whether it was generated by AI. That data travels with the image wherever you post it. Once it's online, you can't take it back.

## What Photo Clean does

Photo Clean scrubs that hidden data from your images so they're safe to share publicly. It removes C2PA content credentials, AI provenance tags, EXIF, XMP, IPTC, and other metadata blocks from JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, and TIFF files.

Just drag your images into the app, click **Run**, and get clean copies back.

## Who it's for

Anyone who shares photos online — real estate agents, marketers, photographers, property managers, small business owners — who wants to make sure their images don't leak location, device, or AI-generation data.

## Privacy & security

- **Runs entirely on your computer** — no images are uploaded anywhere
- **No cloud servers, no accounts, no data leaves your machine**
- Uses exiftool locally to strip metadata
- Verifies after cleaning that no tracking data remains
- You keep full control of your files at all times

## How to use

1. Open Photo Clean
2. Click **Add Files…** or **Add Folder…** to select your images
3. Click **▶ Run — Strip C2PA Metadata**
4. Cleaned images save to your `Downloads/strip-c2pa-out/` folder

Or use the command-line version for batch processing.
